AI Short Generator

AI Short Generator is a personal, single-user desktop automation tool that generates short-form fictional video content (AI-generated horror, mystery, science-fiction, and fantasy stories) and uploads it, with the developer's own authorization, to the developer's own TikTok account draft inbox for manual review and publishing.

This is not a public product or service — it has a single user, its own developer.

AI Short Generator

Last updated: July 2026

AI Short Generator is a personal, single-user desktop automation tool that generates short-form fictional video content (AI-generated horror, mystery, science-fiction, and fantasy stories) and, with the developer's own explicit authorization, uploads that content to the developer's own TikTok account draft inbox for manual review and publishing.

How it works

The application runs a local, end-to-end content pipeline on the developer's own machine:

  • Script generation — an original short story is generated locally using an open-weight language model, in one of four genres (horror, mystery, science-fiction, fantasy).
  • Image generation — a set of illustrative images is generated locally to visually accompany the story.
  • Narration — the script is converted to speech using a local text-to-speech engine.
  • Captioning — captions are generated from the narration audio and synchronized word-by-word.
  • Video assembly — narration, images, captions, and audio are combined into a single vertical video file.

The finished video is then uploaded, under the developer's own TikTok account and explicit authorization, to that account's draft inbox — where the developer manually reviews and decides whether to publish it. No step in this pipeline is publicly hosted or offered as a service to anyone other than the developer.

What this application is not

This is not a public product, platform, or service. It has a single user — its own developer — and is not distributed, marketed, or made available to any other person. It does not create accounts for other users, does not access any TikTok account other than the developer's own, and does not process, request, or store data belonging to any other TikTok user or third party.

Status

This application is actively developed and in day-to-day use by its developer, generating and reviewing new video drafts on an ongoing basis.
